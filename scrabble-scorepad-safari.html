      Settle an argument

      1. Premium squares count once

        A double or triple square scores only on the turn its tile is laid. Later words running through that tile take it at face value.

      2. The centre star doubles the first word

        The star is a double-word square, so the opening play is always worth twice its letters.

      3. A new tile scores in every word it makes

        Lay one tile alongside an existing word and you score each new word formed. The tile counts in all of them, and a premium underneath it applies to each — a double-word square doubles both words through it.
